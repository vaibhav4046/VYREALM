# Showcase

28 finished videos in `outputs/showcase/`, 749 MB, one per distinct format.

Every file was assembled on one Windows machine with an RTX 3050 (6 GB VRAM), with no
cloud service, no API key and no account. Captions come from local Whisper, narration
from local Piper, music from a local ACE-Step model, and the assembly from FFmpeg.

## What these files are, precisely

The format library defines 36 formats across 5 delivery platforms, which expands to
151 renderable variants. **All 151 are cut from 7 source clips totalling about 91
seconds of footage.** Two of those seven are not filmed material at all: they are
generated stills panned for five seconds each.

So the batch was curated down rather than shipped whole. `scripts/curate-showcase.mjs`
scanned 170 rendered files and kept 28:

| Dropped | Count | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Same picture | 70 | Reels / TikTok / Shorts share a 1080x1920 canvas; siblings differ only in caption placement |
| Evidence retimes | 56 | The 53s / 150s / 300s variants are the same cut slowed or ping-ponged |
| Byte-identical | 16 | Five groups of three files with matching content hashes |

Run `node scripts/curate-showcase.mjs` to reproduce that count. It hashes every file
and prints its reasoning.

## Why the showcase is small on purpose

A 151-file catalogue built from 91 seconds of source advertises the repetition, not
the engine. The interesting claim here is not "we made 151 videos" — it is that a
brief becomes a shot plan, a render, timed captions, a sound bed, a 4K thumbnail and
an upload without a single network call, on consumer hardware, with a provenance
receipt attached to every output.

That receipt is the point. `runtime/generation-gate.mjs` refuses to label composited
footage as generated, `runtime/format-render.mjs` stamps
`sourceMethod: 'composited-from-existing-footage'` on every batch file, and
`scripts/render-format-batch.mjs` records `"No pixels were generated by this batch."`
in its own evidence. The system declines to overstate what it made — including here.

## Known limits

- **Source variety is the binding constraint.** More formats do not add more footage.
  Generating genuinely new shots runs at roughly 500–1500 s per shot on this GPU, and
  new keyframes land at `review_required` until a human approves them by design.
- **Native generation resolution is low** (512x288 to 1024x576) and upscaled for
  delivery. Delivery dimensions do not imply native detail.
- **Whisper `tiny.en` is a draft transcriber.** It returns `review_required` and asks
  the operator to check words and timing; it transcribed "Someone" as "some more" in
  our own demo run. Captions are meant to be reviewed, not trusted verbatim.
- **Bulk publishing is deliberately blocked.** `assertNotBulkPublishable` in
  `runtime/format-library.mjs` refuses to mass-upload these recuts, because doing so
  would breach YouTube's policy on repetitive automated content.
