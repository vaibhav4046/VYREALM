# Higgsfield public catalogue → VYREALM benchmarks

Research date: **8 September 2026**. Audience: VYREALM engineering and creative review. Scope: representative public product pages, catalogue labels and official help articles; **not an exhaustive scrape, video-quality measurement, or parity claim**. No reference media, code or assets were downloaded or copied. No paid generation was run.

The useful benchmark is a connected production workflow: reusable references, deliberate shots, selective edits, sound and a finished delivery. A list of model names or 100 project cards does not establish those results.

## What the evidence establishes

- **Observed** below means the public page or official documentation explicitly displayed/described the feature. It does not mean the underlying generation was independently tested. This review did not inspect every catalogue video's frames or audio.
- **Proposed** briefs and gates below are original VYREALM acceptance scenarios, not Higgsfield promises or claims that VYREALM already implements them.
- **Product capability and model capability are different.** Cinema Studio supplies project controls and reusable Elements; its current help article says version 4.0 uses Seedance 2.5. The same article distinguishes Claude-assisted shot planning from the actual generation action. These are disclosed integrations, not proof of a proprietary universal inference model. [Cinema Studio help](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/tools/how-do-i-use-cinema-studio)
- The avatar page lists several video models and voice providers, including Seedance, Veo, Kling, Wan, ElevenLabs and MiniMax. A catalogue category therefore should not be equated with one native model. The page's claims about realism, speed and language coverage remain vendor claims here. [Talking Avatar](https://higgsfield.ai/ai-talking-avatar)
- The Marketing Automation landing page names Seedance 2.0 for video and Soul 2.0 for avatars; Cinema Studio's newer help article names Seedance 2.5. Keep these version/workspace statements separate. They do not establish one model configuration for every tool. [Marketing Automation](https://higgsfield.ai/marketing-automation)
- Higgsfield's MCP page says its ChatGPT integration uses the account's generation credits. MCP is an access interface, not a free local inference backend. VYREALM must keep its own offline workers and provenance when adding agent access. [MCP](https://higgsfield.ai/mcp)

## Apply to VYREALM without overstating progress

These are proposed engineering requirements, not measured Higgsfield internals:

1. Store a versioned creative brief, cast/wardrobe/environment references, per-shot action and camera intent. Inspect the keyframe before spending the GPU budget on motion. Preserve accepted stages when repairing a shot.
2. Separate workflow selection from provider selection. Record exact provider/model/workflow hashes, input references, seed, native dimensions/fps, generated outputs, edit lineage and enhancement method. A provider's unavailable feature stays blocked or receives an explicitly different fallback label.
3. For every reviewed film, retain complete playable source and delivery clips, one-frame-per-second contact sheets and a written review tied to the actual output hash. Watch complete motion and listen to complete audio; contact sheets alone cannot establish temporal quality or lip sync.
4. Apply technical gates to frame count, requested duration, decode errors, black/corrupt frames, caption bounds, audio synchronisation and final delivery dimensions. Keep subject realism and continuity judgments distinct from those technical checks; do not invent a universal numerical quality score.
5. On the 6 GB / 16 GB target, qualify one short shot per route first and record measured wall time and memory. Run one heavy job at a time. 1080p/4K resizing or interpolation must retain their true source-resolution and source-frame-rate labels.
6. Begin catalogue selection with a few independently reviewed original films covering different capabilities. A saved plan, duplicate aspect-ratio export or procedural thumbnail is not another generated flagship film. Scale production only after the route passes repeatable end-to-end checks.

## Evidence gaps and stopping point

The inspected pages support these twelve representative categories and the explicit provider/workspace distinctions. They do **not** expose Higgsfield's full private architecture, training data, inference harness, failure rate, cost structure or complete catalogue inventory. No independent virality study, blind visual comparison, frame-by-frame media audit or benchmark on the Lenovo laptop was conducted in this document. Exact visual parity remains unestablished.

Discovery used the public homepage, its catalogue/tool links, targeted official-site searches, then help/blog follow-ups for Cinema Studio, marketing, anime, avatars and Genjutsu. Research stopped when every representative row had a primary source and the material unknowns above were explicit. All linked sources are Higgsfield first-party pages, accessed on the research date; the Cinema Studio and Marketing Studio help pages displayed **1 August 2026**. Undated landing-page claims are access-dated, not assumed current implementation measurements.
