Your footage.

VYREALM helps you turn imported footage into revisioned edits, captions and rendered video files on your computer.

Windows releases Read the source →

Edit, review, export.

Upload footage into the local app, describe supported edits, refine the timeline, then render and review the result. Templates prepare project plans. Local automations can render a saved edit and prepare creator materials for your review.

The Windows application and browser interface share a loopback API and local project store. Optional model runtimes require separate installation and compatible hardware. Cloud inference is not the default.

Connect your own YouTube channel, optionally.

Configured Google OAuth can authorize channel readback, read-only analytics and an explicitly confirmed upload of a reviewed video. The published integration limits uploads to private visibility. Google receives the selected video and metadata when you upload; normal local editing does not upload media to Google.

Google connection availability depends on the configured OAuth client, consent and Google project restrictions. These pages do not mean Google has verified VYREALM or that every Google account can connect. Public release qualification is described in the repository; code availability is not proof of a successful live upload.

Read how your data is handled and the terms of use before connecting.
