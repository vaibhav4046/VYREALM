## Original treatment

**Between Two Armies** is a separate 30-second, six-shot interpretation of Arjuna's hesitation and Krishna's counsel at Kurukshetra. Its dawn setting, wardrobe designs and precise actions are artistic choices, not claims about an archaeologically established historical setting. See SCRIPT_RESEARCH.md for the textual sources.

Keep the visual language restrained: warm dawn side light, muted brass and saffron, physically coherent timber chariot, dry soil, natural skin and woven cloth. One readable action per five-second shot. No supplied-reference face copying, giant spectacle fabricated from primitives, decorative rings, or vector fallback.

## Acceptance record required before flagship selection

- Local provider prompt and model digest, keyframe and motion workflow hashes, owned frame ledger and output hashes.
- Full 30-second playable 24fps delivery, real soundtrack, exact 720 frames, correct project/timeline ownership.
- One-frame-per-second contact sheet plus closer temporal samples of faces, hands and moving objects.
- Explicit review of subject detail, identity, anatomy, movement, environment, lighting, continuity and sound alignment; identify faults rather than inventing a numerical quality score.
- Exact source and delivery resolution labels. A 1024×576 source expanded to 1920×1080 is not native 1080p generation. Enhancement does not establish parity with the reference.
- Only a passed, hash-bound review of the final assembled film can enter the flagship catalogue. Plans, incomplete shots and failed footage remain outside it.
